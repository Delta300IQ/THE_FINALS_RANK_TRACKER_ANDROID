# THE FINALS rank tracker for Android 
I created an Android app to keep track of your ranked stats all along the seasons in THE FINALS. That's my first app so it's far from being perfect but it works. Also I don't pretend to be a developer, actual dev would cry over my code, I used AI to create it because I barely know anything in coding, hope you will understand and not hate on me. I used the following AI : Gemini, Chatgpt, Claude, Kimi K3, Z AI, Google AI studio and Google antigravity.

All your data stays locally on your phone, no account needed, no ads, and no tracking. An internet connection is only needed if you choose to use the Top 10,000 auto-import feature or the update checker.This is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Embark Studios in any way. 

# Features
- **Bilingual** (French / English)
- **Rank Tracking** — Log your Rank Score manually after every match or let the app do it automatically.
- **Top 10,000 Auto-Import** — Automatically fetch and sync your match history and stats directly from the Top 10,000 leaderboard (powered by davg25.com).
- **Interactive Charts** — Visualize your progression with an interactive, zoomable chart. Choose your favorite style: Candlestick, Straight Line, or Curved Line.
- **Detailed Statistics** — Average gain/loss, win rate, best/worst streaks, peak, lowest ranks and much more.
- **Match Notes** — Tag matches with contextual notes (SoloQ, DuoQ, good/bad teammates, etc.) and analyze your average performance per tag.
- **Goal Setter** — Define a target rank and get an estimate of how many matches remain based on your current pace.
- **Season Comparison** — Compare your performance across different seasons side-by-side.
- **Performance by Time & Heatmap** — Discover whether you play better at night, in the morning, or on specific days of the week, complete with an interactive activity heatmap.
- **Anti-Tilt Warning** — A friendly reminder screen to take a break when a losing streak is detected.
- **Import / Export** — Backup and restore your database via JSON file.
- **In-App Update Checker** — Get notified when a new version is released on GitHub.
